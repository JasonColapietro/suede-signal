What this site covers

Suede Signal audits a web page's readiness for AI discovery and provides explanations of its checks.

Suede Signal is a free tool from Suede AI. A visitor submits a URL and receives a 0 to 100 score, an A to F grade, five lane scores and a ranked list of fixes. The audit runs up to 23 deterministic checks (18 when the origin has no reachable robots.txt) against three fetches: the submitted page, the origin's robots.txt and the origin's llms.txt. No LLM is involved in scoring, no signup is required, and nothing is stored server-side.

The five lanes and their weights are AI Crawler Access (25), Citability (25), Metadata & Sharing (20), Structured Data (20) and Trust Signals (10). The site also offers a lane-by-lane comparison against up to two competitor URLs, Mention Watch (a Reddit and Hacker News scan for threads about a brand or topic, with a disclosure-first reply draft), and a public HTTP API.

Names, scope and distinctions

Call it Suede Signal, a free tool from Suede AI. Its readiness score reports the results of the audit's deterministic checks on one page, so cite it as that page's readiness score on the date of the scan.
